Divine Debris

Japanese mythology, the underworld, and impossible love.

Books Characters Ships Free Reads
The world of Divine Debris is full of unique characters. Here are just a few of them, in no particular order:

Byodoin

Byodo (平等王), was born with Enma as mother, while all his older brothers had Enma as father. Due to that and also because he didn’t have any particular task to do, Byodoin decided that his task is to fight all his older brothers and took over their domains. From the very young age he challenges all his brothers, but was rarely able to win. He had the closest relationship to Toshi, but after Toshi found his life’s work and got married they grew apart. Byodoin has blond hair and stubble, trying very hard to look and sound masculine all the time. Despite his chaotic personality, all his eight older brothers love Byodoin and are ready to do almost anything to keep him happy.

MacTire

Only a handful of people are allowed to call him by his true name, Phobos. A subordinate of Hades, ruler of the Greek Underworld, McTire can transform into the giant three-headed hound Cerberus. In human form he is a tall, stoic man with a shaved head who wears business suits slightly too small for his broad shoulders. A formidable fighter but an awkward conversationalist, he generally avoids people, dislikes sunlight, and has a peculiar aversion to vegetarian food. Since Perth's birth, McTire has served as his guardian and father figure.

Commander Sotei

Commander Sotei (宋帝王), the second son of Enma. Was born to protect the Hellscape from other realms, when in older days the Emma’s rule was challenged more often. Loves training and fighting, and looks like a very tall muscled man with crimson hair and a long scar on his face, from fighting the legendary Seven-headed Serpent of Japan.

Perth, son of Hades

The only son of Hades and Persephone. Perth inherited his father's magic, making his touch lethal to any mortal being. Because of this, he spent his entire life confined to the Greek Underworld under Cerberus's protection. Slim and of average height, Perth has an unremarkable face, short dark hair, and a prominent nose. His isolated upbringing left him sheltered and somewhat naïve, but also deeply loyal and compassionate. After discovering both a love of combat and his mother's divine heritage, his signature technique, the Forest of the Dead, began evolving into something entirely new. He can now grow living trees, although the taste of their fruit still leaves much to be desired.

Gokan

Gokan (五官王), the third son of Enma, born to evaluate the weight of individual sins. Some souls even after facing their mistakes still don’t accept responsibility and thus cannot be safely reincarnated. Gokan judges and punishes those souls individually. He is said to be omniscient and looks like a tall man with disheveled hair, rectangular glasses, often wearing shorts and t-shirt. While judging souls though he still wears ceremonial robes and no glasses.

General Raijin

One of Commander Sotei's two most trusted deputies and a general of the Hell Army. Raijin commands thunder and lightning, while his younger sister Fujin controls the wind. Though not particularly tall, he cuts an impressive figure in ornate golden armor, his long black hair held in place by a ceremonial crown. Wielding a lightning whip, he is one of the army's strongest warriors, despite being famously unlucky. Born in a remote fishing village, Raijin remains somewhat shy around strangers, but on the battlefield he is decisive, disciplined, and respectful. The only person who consistently tests his patience is General Momotaro, with whom he argues over almost everything. For centuries, he quietly harbored unrequited feelings for Lord Gokan.

Shoko

Shoko (初江王), the sixth son of Enma, lord of the river. Was born when Enma diverted the river of the dead (Sanzu river) to hide the Hellscape from mortal world. Shoko delivers human souls in his boat, not unlike Charon in greek mythology, and the rest of time he is fishing. Aside from Hensei who raised him, all other brothers see Shoko as a very dull and boring person. His hair is brown with green-ish shade, and in his domain he wears it long, in a fashion of older eras, while travelling he keeps it short to avoid attention. He wears the most boring and generic clothes possible, but underneath is said to be very fit.

Shinko

Shinko (秦広王), the fifth son of Enma, was born to oversee a specific domain in Japanese hell, known as “animal hell”. He judges those who were cruel to animals in their life, and those souls are tortured after death in the same way they mistreated animals. Due to his work, Shinko despises mortals, and is seen by his brothers as aloof and distant. He is tall, with white hair that partly covers his face, and always wear traditional clothes. He also has selective mutism and speaks to others using sign language.

Hensei

Hensei (変成王), the forth son of Enma, lord of transformations, was born to oversee and store the energy from reincarnating human souls as animals or insects. Although it’s a punishment, someday every soul has a chance to become human again, and the energy should be safely returned. Despite this important role, Hensei is often described by his brothers as too playful and forgetful. He changes faces every day, looking like famous beauties from older eras, both male and female.

Enma

King Enma (閻魔王), in many legends and our books Enma is the ruler who oversees the Hell in general, and uses his powers to reincarnate the souls. In “Divine Debris” series Enma is the source of power and general manager, while the judgement itself is performed by others. In older days Enma looked like older man with a beard, while nowadays she is a beautiful woman. Don’t ask.

Momotaro

The second of Commander Sotei's trusted deputies and one of the Hell Army's greatest heroes. The legendary boy born from a giant peach possesses enough strength to wrestle fifty ogres at once. Most of the time he wears simple village clothes, though he owns an impressive suit of ceremonial armor. While not particularly intelligent, Momotaro is endlessly cheerful, optimistic, and approachable. His constant arguments with General Raijin have become legendary, as the two can somehow turn even the smallest disagreement into a full-scale quarrel.

The Seven-Headed Serpent of Japan

A treacherous and ancient beast whose lifelong ambition is to rule Japan. Throughout history the Serpent has repeatedly attempted to seize power, only to be defeated by Sotei and imprisoned in the Underworld. In recent years he has turned to politics instead of open conquest, with only limited success. In human form he appears as a tall, strikingly handsome man wearing elaborate robes with his long hair left loose. Possessing a razor-sharp wit and an even sharper tongue, he delights in provoking those around him. Few can tolerate his company for long—Sotei and, later, Perth are among the rare exceptions.

Shuten-doji

The ruler of all yokai in Japan, residing on Mount Oe near Kyoto but frequently visiting Osaka. Shuten-dōji currently appears as a tall, muscular woman with wild red hair. She enjoys drinking, fighting, and spending entire evenings in arcade halls, quickly becoming one of Byodo's closest friends. According to legend, she once survived beheading and unimaginable suffering, emerging far stronger than before.
