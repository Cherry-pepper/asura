Divine Debris

Japanese mythology, the underworld, and impossible love.

Books Characters Ships Free Reads
The cast of characters in the Divine Debris world includes , here are some of them in no particular order:

Byodoin

Byodo (平等王), was born with Enma as mother, while all his older brother had Enma as father. Due to that and also because he didn’t have any particular task to do, Byodoin decided that his task is to fight all his older brothers and took over their domains. From the very young age he challenges all his brothers, but was rarely able to win. He had the closest relationship to Toshi, but after Toshi found his life’s work and got married they grew apart. Byodoin has blond hair and stubble, trying very hard to look and sound masculine all the time. Despite his chaotic personality, all his eight older brothers love Byodoin and are ready to do almost anything to keep him happy.

MacTire

Only a handful of people are allowed to call him by his true name, Phobos. A subordinate of Hades, ruler of the Greek Underworld, McTire can transform into the giant three-headed hound Cerberus. In human form he is a tall, stoic man with a shaved head who wears business suits slightly too small for his broad shoulders. A formidable fighter but an awkward conversationalist, he generally avoids people, dislikes sunlight, and has a peculiar aversion to vegetarian food. Since Perth's birth, McTire has served as his guardian and father figure.

Commander Sotei

Commander Sotei (宋帝王), the second son of Enma. Was born to protect the Hellscape from other realms, when in older days the Emma’s rule was challenged more often. Loves training and fighting, and looks like a very tall muscled man with crimson hair and a long scar on his face, from fighting the legendary Seven-headed Serpent of Japan.

Perth, son of Hades

The only son of Hades and Persephone. Perth inherited his father's magic, making his touch lethal to any mortal being. Because of this, he spent his entire life confined to the Greek Underworld under Cerberus's protection. Slim and of average height, Perth has an unremarkable face, short dark hair, and a prominent nose. His isolated upbringing left him sheltered and somewhat naïve, but also deeply loyal and compassionate. After discovering both a love of combat and his mother's divine heritage, his signature technique, the Forest of the Dead, began evolving into something entirely new. He can now grow living trees, although the taste of their fruit still leaves much to be desired.
