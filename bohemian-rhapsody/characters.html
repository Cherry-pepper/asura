Lotty

I said Lotty is gay, but that’s an understatement. Imagine Colombo levels of obnoxiousness, but instead of his wife, Lotty talks about his boyfriends.
As I mentioned, I’m a photographer, and I work with Lotty on his cases. I run errands for him. Stalk his suspects when he’s getting a manicure. It would be nice to imagine myself as a Dr. Watson, especially now that I have no choice but to write this. But no, I’m more Archie Goodwin to his Nero Wolfe. Only Archie was fed nice French cuisine for his troubles. I’d rather die than eat anything Lotty consumes on a daily basis. Even when he dated a chef, he still ate crap fast food.
