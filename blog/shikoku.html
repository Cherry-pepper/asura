Shikoku journey, April 2025

TLDR: Shikoku has so much to offer, it's not crowded and the food is beyond delicious!

Day 1

Osaka → Kochi

8:00 Highway bus to Kochi
Around 15:30 arrival in Kochi

Kochi
Katsurahama Park, a scenic seaside park with a long sandy beach, pine trees and views over the Pacific

Kochi city → hotel
Stay at Toyoko Inn

Day2

Ioki cave

Kochi → Ioki
Tosa Kuroshio Railway, around 1 hour

Around 9:00 arrival at Ioki

Ioki Cave
A short cave and fern-covered canyon formed by ancient sea erosion, with fossils in the rock walls

Ioki Cave → coastline
Walk along the coast

Lunch at a cafe overlooking the sea

Ioki → Kochi
Afternoon train, around 15:00–16:00

Return to Kochi

Day 3

Kochi → Utazu

Kochi → Miyoshi
Morning train, around 7:00–9:00

Miyoshi / Oboke area

Miyoshi → Oboke Gorge
Bus, around 10:00

Oboke Gorge
Dramatic gorge carved through the mountains by the Yoshino River

Oboke Gorge boat ride
Sightseeing boat through the gorge

Around 13:00 bus toward Nishi-Iya / Nishiiyamamura Ichiu Park

Nishiiyamamura Ichiu Park: Obstacle course and zipline in the mountain park

Around 16:00–17:00 bus back toward Miyoshi

Miyoshi → Utazu
Limited Express, around 17:00–19:00

Stay in Utazu

Day 4

Utazu → Takamatsu

Morning
Explore Utazu, walk around the town and try local seafood

Seto Ohashi Park
Waterfront park with views of the Seto Ohashi Bridge and the Seto Inland Sea

Afternoon
Cinema: A3! stage live translation
Utazu → Takamatsu
JR train, around 20–30 minutes

Takamatsu Park and Takamatsu Castle
Historic castle site built beside the sea, known for its unusual surviving seawater moat

Takamatsu Castle
Home of the Nikkari Aoi sword, well known through Touken Ranbu game

Stay in Takamatsu

Day 5

Takamatsu → Tsuda

8:00–8:20
Takamatsu → Megijima

Megijima, also known as Oni Island or Onigashima, is associated with the Japanese legend of Momotaro and is famous for its large cave complex.
Explore the island and Oni Cave

Around 12:00 / 13:00 ferry back to Takamatsu

Megijima → Takamatsu
Around 13:20–13:40

Takamatsu → Tsuda
JR train, around 1 hour

Around 16:00–17:00 arrival

Stay at seaside ryokan, relax in onsen

Day 6

Tsuda → Osaka


Morning
Jakomaru Park, Tsuda

Tsuda no Matsubara
Historic pine forest stretching along the coast

Tsuda → Sanuki
JR train, around 30 minutes

Sanuki → Osaka
Highway bus

Afternoon / evening arrival in Osaka
