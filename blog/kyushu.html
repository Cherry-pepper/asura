Kyushu journey April 2026

TLDR: A very relaxing journey in search of vulcanos and ancient gods. Tsubame shinkansen has a very unconvenient schedule.

Day 1

Osaka → Kagoshima


Early morning flight, Osaka KIX → Kagoshima
Around 7:00–8:20

Kagoshima Airport → Kagoshima city
Around 9:00–10:00

Kagoshima Port → Sakurajima
Ferry, around 10:00–10:15

Sakurajima
Explore the volcanic island and the coastline around the active volcano

Ferry back to Kagoshima
Around 15:00–15:15

Kagoshima → Yoshinocho
Bus, around 16:00–17:00

Stay at hostel in Yoshinocho
Evening at the beach, looking across Kagoshima Bay at Sakurajima
The Sakurajima ferry crossing takes about 15 minutes and operates very frequently.

Day 2

Kagoshima


Yoshinocho → Kagoshima city
Bus, around 9:00

Kagoshima City Kids Literature Museum
Around 10:00–14:00

Spend a long afternoon at the museum, then walk around central Kagoshima and visit bookstores

Kagoshima city → Yoshinocho
Bus, around 17:00–18:00

Evening, heavy rain

Day 3

Kagoshima → Kumamoto


Yoshinocho → Kagoshima-Chuo / Kagoshima Station
Around 8:00–9:00

Kagoshima-Chuo → Kumamoto
Kyushu Shinkansen, around 9:00–10:00
Approximately 1 hour

The original plan was to take Tsubame, but the train was a different Kyushu Shinkansen service

Kumamoto
Kumamoto Castle, one of Kyushu’s most famous castles, with extensive stone walls and historic grounds

Around 11:00–16:00, explore Kumamoto Castle and central Kumamoto

Stay at Toyoko Inn

Day 4

Kumamoto → Minamiaso


Kagoshima / Kumamoto area → Minamiaso
Early morning train connection

Minamiaso Railway → Aso / Takamori area
Around 8:00–10:00

Bus to Mt. Aso
Around 10:00

Mt. Aso
Explore the volcanic landscape, crater area and surrounding peaks

All-day volcano exploration using the unlimited bus pass

Around 16:00–17:00 return toward Minamiaso

Stay at homestay in Nakamatsu, Minamiaso

Day 5

Nakamatsu → Takachiho


Nakamatsu → Takamori
Morning train / local connection
Around 8:00–9:00

Takamori → Takachiho
Bus, approximately 50 minutes

Around 10:00–11:00 arrival

Takachiho Gorge
Dramatic volcanic gorge with steep cliffs, river and waterfalls

Explore the gorge

Takachiho Amaterasu Railway
Scenic ride along the former railway line, including the high bridge over the gorge

Late afternoon, return to Takachiho

Day 6

Takamori → Miyazaki


Takamori → Miyazaki
Morning train and bus connections

Around 8:00–9:00 departure

Takamori → Takachiho / Miyazaki area
Bus connections

Miyazaki
Around 14:00–16:00 arrival

Stay at Toyoko Inn

Miyazaki → Aoshima
Train, around 30 minutes

Aoshima
Small tropical island with Aoshima Shrine and the unusual wave-carved rocks of the Oni no Sentakuita

Return to Miyazaki

Miyazaki → Aoshima
Morning train

Possible current morning services include 7:20, 7:53, 8:09 and 8:32 from Aoshima toward Miyazaki on the return journey.

Morning at Aoshima
Aoshima Shrine, beach and surrounding coastline

Aoshima → Miyazaki
Around 11:00–12:00

Miyazaki → Miyazaki Airport
Around 13:00–14:00

Flight Miyazaki → Osaka KIX
Around 16:00
