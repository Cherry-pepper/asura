Come join the Divine Debris Street Team!

I’m looking for readers who enjoy the books and would like to be a little more involved in the world: sharing new releases, talking about characters and ships, taking part in occasional reader activities, or simply hanging around and chatting about Japanese Hell.

I’m not looking for a professional marketing team, and there are no quotas or obligations. I’d mostly like to meet people who genuinely enjoy the books and would like to be part of the community around them.

And since I live in Osaka, there may occasionally be some real-world perks too: recommendations for your trip to Japan, food and sightseeing tips, and, if our schedules happen to line up, perhaps even coffee or a little tour around Osaka.

If that sounds like something you'd enjoy, fill in the form and come say hello.
