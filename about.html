About me

Hello, I’m Lia Asura, an M/M romance author living in Osaka, Japan. I write urban fantasy with a dash of humor, lots of food, and a particular interest in relationships between Japanese people and foreigners, both romantic and otherwise.

My favorite authors include Tolkien, Le Guin, Pratchett, Heinlein, Doyle, and Agatha Christie, while my storytelling is heavily inspired by Doctor Who. I like my stories to mix mystery, action, and romance, with Japanese folklore, oni, yokai, and ancient legends often making an appearance. I have visited many places connected with these traditions, including sites associated with Abe no Seimei.

I’m not neurotypical, so before I started writing I obtained a PhD in medicine. But the greatest joy in my life has always been reading, and when I write, I’m first and foremost trying to create the kind of stories I want to read myself. And I love HEAs, healthy relationships, power couples, and a good mystery to solve.
